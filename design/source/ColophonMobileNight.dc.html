How this notebook was built

This notebook is a Next.js site in TypeScript and Tailwind. Every version is one MDX file: the facts page renders from its frontmatter, the story from its body.

The page curl is mine, not a library. Drag a corner and the fold is the perpendicular bisector between where the corner started and where your pointer is. The page is clipped along that line, and the flap is the same page reflected across it. The text stays real, selectable DOM.

a fold is a bisector ↑afoldisabisector↑

Wheel, drag, keys and clicks all write one shared progress value, from 0 to 1.

details:
  - label: "Problem"
    text: "I had photographs from thirteen countries and nowhere to put them that wasn't a grid of thumbnails. Where a travel photograph was taken is half of what it is, so I wanted the map to be the index."
  - label: "Role"
    text: "Solo — design, code, data pipeline, photo processing and deploy."
  - label: "Key decisions"
    text: "One canvas renders everything, globe and flat map both; an earlier plan drew the settled map as SVG, which meant keeping two renderers in sync for no gain. Picking paints every country offscreen in a colour encoding its id, then reads the pixel under the cursor. The morph blends the two projection formulas rather than swapping projections, so there is no seam."
  - label: "Rejected idea"
    text: "A real 3D globe: harder to hit-test a country, and it buys nothing the blended 2D projection doesn't. The map is cut from phones too — at 390px seven lit European countries fall inside a 29x23px box, so nothing there is tappable."
  - label: "Result"
    text: "Nine countries and 53 photographs are published; four more are built but parked until I shoot enough frames. The dot field is baked at build time — 176 countries, 8,522 dots, 266KB — so the browser never does geometry."
